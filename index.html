ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Dr. Tina McMechen

Dr. Tina McMechen lives with her husband, Matt McMechen, and family in Arkansas. She has three amazing children, Vivian, Harrison, and Laura.     COMMON QUERIES

    Frequently Answered Concerns

    The Sandwich Generation refers to adults who are simultaneously caring for their aging parents (typically over 65) while raising their own dependent children (typically under 18 or in college). They are literally 'sandwiched' in the middle of two highly demanding generations requiring care, attention, and financial support.
